# Employee Analytics Project

This end-to-end project focuses on analyzing **employee performance, satisfaction, and retention trends** using **Google Cloud Platform’s BigQuery** and visualizing insights through a **dynamic Looker dashboard**.

---

## Tech Stack & Approach

- **BigQuery** – Cloud-based storage, transformation, and querying of large datasets.
- **SQL & ETL Processes** – Cleaned and transformed raw employee data, merged multiple datasets, calculated KPIs, and prepared it for visualization.
- **Looker** – Designed an interactive dashboard highlighting key metrics, trends, and departmental insights.

---

## Key Insights

- **Salary & Education**: Employees with advanced degrees (Masters/Doctorate) are often in higher salary brackets.
- **Attrition Trends**: Attrition is higher in younger age groups (18–31) and varies by department and job satisfaction.
- **Training & Performance**: Employees who take more training opportunities tend to exceed performance expectations.
- **Manager Influence**: Years with the current manager correlate strongly with satisfaction and performance ratings.
- **Work-Life Balance Impact**: Employee satisfaction closely ties to work-life balance across departments.
